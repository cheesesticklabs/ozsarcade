Rogue Dino Catcher

Move the mouse to the screen edges to turn. Click to throw a ball and add a creature to your team. Battered creatures are easy to catch.

Switch to Send partner (key 3) and click a wild creature to fight it with your lead. Winners gain XP and level up. In the team list, Fuse a spare creature into your lead for a big XP boost. Rock (key 2) provokes wild fights.
