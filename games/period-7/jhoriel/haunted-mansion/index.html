🧭 Player controls

Player 1 — Blue explorer: move with WASD. Press F to reveal nearby hidden objects for 4 seconds.

Player 2 — Amber explorer: move with ↑←↓→. Press Enter to sprint for 2 seconds.

Both players can press Space to create a ghost distraction that lasts 3 seconds.

👻 How to escape

Collect all 3 keys, then get at least one player to the green exit.

Touching a ghost adds a team capture and sends that player back to the entrance. Three captures means game over.

Watch for red floor traps. The room layout changes every round, so explore together!

Ready when you are. Press Start / Restart Game.

First-draft tip: the mansion is a small grid of rooms connected by passages. Keys are hidden in different rooms each round; hold your nerve and keep moving!
