SPIKE DASH

Dodge the spikes. Reach the flag. Beat your friend!

VS

How to play

  • Two players share one keyboard and race through the same level.
  • Touch a spike and you lose a life and restart the level. Harder levels give you fewer lives (5, then 4, 3, and just 2 on level 10).
  • First one to the flag wins the level. There are 10 levels, each harder than the last.
  • Win the most levels to win the game!

Controls

Player 1A D move   W jump
Player 2← → move   Space jump
