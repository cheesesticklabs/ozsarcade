Memorize four symbols. Find them in order, then reach the exit. The hand accelerates as you collect sigils. The room stays almost completely dark; blackouts reduce your sight to a tiny glow.

MEMORY

Collect all four sigils in the shown order. Wrong order resets your progress.

SURVIVE

Hide in a shadow patch when the hand is near. Reach the green exit after collecting all four sigils.

Prototype note: stay inside the room walls. Shadow patches are the dark circular areas; the hand cannot catch players hiding inside them.
