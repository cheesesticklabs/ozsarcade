Escape Mr Pino

It is after hours. The school is locked, two floors are open, and Mr Pino is walking the halls looking for you.

  • Run, sprint and break his line of sight.
  • Step up to a blue locker and press E to hide. He can't find you if he doesn't see you go in.
  • When the screen turns red you have 10 seconds to reach a green safe room on either floor.
  • Survive 5 red alerts to get out.
