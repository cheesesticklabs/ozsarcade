Escape Mr Pino

You are alone in a huge two-floor school after hours, and Mr Pino is hunting you. If he catches you, it is game over.

Collect the 3 hall passes (one is upstairs), then reach the green EXIT on the first floor. Hide in lockers when he cannot see you.

Watch for the red alert. When the screen turns red you have 10 seconds to reach a green SAFE ROOM, on either floor.

W A S D move. Mouse or the arrow keys look around (click the game for mouse look). Hold Shift to sneak. E or Space hides in a locker or takes the stairs.
