⚽ Derby Day: Dribble, Pass, Score

Move: WASD / arrows · Aim: mouse (touch: tap the right half of the pitch, drag the left half to move) · Shoot: Space or click (tap again right after for a curve; from the opponent half it usually goes in) · Switch player: click a team-mate or press Q · Pass: X (goes to the green-ringed team-mate, the one closest to where you aim; then you control them) · Ball out of play = throw-in, corner or goal kick
