OPERATION DEADDROP

You are dropped in with an M9 pistol and full health. Hostiles are patrolling. Scavenge the map: basic weapons lie on the ground, but the best gear is locked in supply crates. Crates are hidden, so find them by ear. Each one sends out a beacon that gets louder as you close in and pans toward its direction.

Standard crate: short double beep, holds uncommon weapons (green) and very rarely a legendary. Elite crate: rising three-tone pulse, much quieter range, always holds a legendary weapon (gold). Floor weapons are common.

WASD move · mouse aim · click fire · R reload · E open crate · Q or 1-9 switch weapon. Pick up medkits (red cross) to heal, and health also regenerates slowly out of fire.
