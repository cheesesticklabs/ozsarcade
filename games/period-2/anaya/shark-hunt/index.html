👻 HAUNTED SHARK HUNT 🦈

🔴 Player 1: 0 fish 🔵 Player 2: 0 fish 🐟 Sharks fed: 0/10 ⏱️ Time: 2:30
Player 1: Move with W A S D, feed with F.   Player 2: Move with I J K L, feed with O.
Collect glowing fish, then get close to the shark and press your feed key. The shark chases one player at a time and switches targets after each feeding. Feed it 10 times before the timer reaches zero to win!
Tip: work together and keep moving. If the shark catches you, you drop one fish.
