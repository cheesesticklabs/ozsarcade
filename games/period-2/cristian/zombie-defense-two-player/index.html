ZOMBIE DEFENSE

Team up, shoot zombies, collect coins, and build defenses. Can you survive all 20 rounds?

Player 1: WASD to move, F to shoot, G to place selected defense.
Player 2: Arrow keys to move, Enter to shoot, Right Shift to place defense.
Press Space to pause/resume.

🛒 Defense Shop — buy with coins

Pick an item, buy it, then press G (P1) or Right Shift (P2) near your base to place it.

🎮 How to Play

Player 1 — Move: WASD · Shoot: F · Place defense: G
Player 2 — Move: Arrow keys · Shoot: Enter · Place defense: Right Shift
Mouse: Move the mouse over the arena to aim P1; press F to fire.

• Zombies chase the players and get tougher each round.
• Each zombie drops coins when defeated. Walk over them to collect them.
• Buy walls to block zombies, electric gates to damage them, and turrets to help shoot.
• Both players must survive to reach Round 20. If either player is down, the game ends.
